Coil is a neon serpent game about choosing a clean line through a crowded grid. Guide the head with a turn before the next beat and watch the luminous trail bend behind it.

A pellet adds a segment to your coil. The border and your own body are deadly, so every bite makes the route tighter and every turn matters.

Eat again before the combo window drains to raise your multiplier, up to x5. Keep a rhythm between pellets to turn a careful run into a high score.
